Homework 11

Euler-Maruyama & The Wiener Process

1. Theory: Stochastic Differential Equations (SDE)

We solve an SDE of the form:

$dX_t = \mu(t, X_t)dt + \sigma(t, X_t)dW_t$

Where $W_t$ is a Wiener process (Brownian Motion). To simulate this, we use the Euler-Maruyama method, a generalization of the Euler method for ODEs.

2. Discretization

$X_{t+\Delta t} = X_t + \mu \Delta t + \sigma \sqrt{\Delta t} \cdot Z_t$

Where $Z_t \sim N(0,1)$ is a standard normal random variable generated via the Box-Muller Transform.
