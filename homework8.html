Bernoulli, Random Walks & Combinatorial Structures

1. Analogies: HW7 (Random Walk) vs HW8 (LLN/Bernoulli)

We analyze the deep mathematical connections between the Server Security trajectory (Random Walk) and the classic Bernoulli Process leading to the Law of Large Numbers (LLN).

HW7: Server Security (Random Walk)

  • Process: Cumulative sum of random steps $X_i \in \{-1, +1\}$.
  • Variable: $S_n = \sum X_i$ (Final Score).
  • Space: Range from $-n$ to $+n$.
  • Perspective: Focus on the path (trajectory) and diffusion over time.
  • Limit: Converges to Normal Distribution $N(0, n)$ (for p=0.5). Variance grows with $n$.

HW8: Bernoulli / LLN

  • Process: Sum of successes $Y_i \in \{0, 1\}$.
  • Variable: $K_n = \sum Y_i$ (Count of Successes) or Mean $\bar{Y}_n$.
  • Space: Range from $0$ to $n$.
  • Perspective: Focus on the proportion (frequency) converging to probability $p$.
  • Limit: Relative frequency converges to $p$. Variance of the mean shrinks ($p(1-p)/n$).

The Core Connection: Both are fundamentally Bernoulli Trials.
If we map $\{0, 1\} \to \{-1, +1\}$ via the transformation $X = 2Y - 1$, the Random Walk is simply a shifted and scaled version of the Binomial count. The underlying combinatorics are identical.

2. Mathematical Structures

A. Binomial Coefficients & Pascal's Triangle

The number of ways to reach a specific score $k$ in a Random Walk (or $k$ successes in Bernoulli) is given by the Binomial Coefficient $\binom{n}{k}$.

This creates Pascal's Triangle. Each number is the sum of the two directly above it, exactly matching the logic of a Random Walk: to get to position $x$ at time $t$, you must have come from $x-1$ or $x+1$ at time $t-1$.

B. The Fibonacci Connection

While Pascal's triangle sums rows to $2^n$, summing the diagonals of Pascal's triangle yields the Fibonacci Sequence ($1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8...$). This connects probability paths to recursive growth patterns.
