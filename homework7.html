Server Security Trajectories & Binomial Convergence

1. Theoretical Framework

We model a server receiving weekly security updates. For each week i (step), there is an outcome X_i based on whether an attacker breaches the system.

1. The Probability Space:
Let p be the probability of a breach (failure).
Let q = 1 - p be the probability of remaining secure (success).
2. The Random Walk (Score):
If secure: Score +1.
If breached: Score -1.
The cumulative score after n weeks is: S_n = Sum(X_i) for i=1 to n.
3. Relation to Binomial Distribution:
Let k be the number of secure weeks (successes). Then n-k is the number of breaches.
The final score is: S_n = (+1)*k + (-1)*(n-k) = k - n + k = 2k - n.
Since k follows a Binomial distribution B(n, 1-p), the Score S_n is a linear transformation of a Binomial variable.
4. Convergence:
As n and m (number of attackers/trajectories) diverge, the distribution of scores approaches a Normal distribution (by the Central Limit Theorem) centered at n*(1-2p).
